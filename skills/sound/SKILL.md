# Sound

Sound should be felt, not noticed: the voice is the star, the music is a warm floor under it,
and effects mark structure. `ww stitch` does the mixing; you choose settings in `plan.sound`:

```json
"sound": {"music": "calm", "music_db": -17, "sfx": true, "intro": true, "outro": true}
```

## Music
- `music` is a mood, a file path, or `"none"`. Moods are generated procedurally by
  `scripts/music.py` at the exact length of the video (so no loop seams), seeded by the title:
  - `calm`: warm major-7th pad. Default; math, explanations, most topics.
  - `curious`: minor-9th pad with a soft pluck arpeggio. Mysteries, puzzles, "wait, why?".
  - `bright`: lydian pad with plucks. Products, companies, upbeat or playful topics.
  - `focus`: sparse and low. Dense technical material, serious subjects.
  - `none`: grief, tragedy, or when the user asks.
- A file path loops that file (fade in 2 s, out 3 s). Use only music whose licence you have
  verified; record it in the job's `plan.sources`. `assets/music/` ships 60 s previews of the four
  moods (generated by this repo, MIT); stitch renders a fresh bed at the exact length anyway.
- If the user explicitly asks for bespoke music and has ElevenLabs credits, the MCP's
  `compose_music` can make a track; save it into `workspace/` and pass its path as `music`.
- `music_db` (default -17) is the bed's level *before* ducking. While the voice speaks,
  `sidechaincompress` (threshold 0.02, ratio 8, attack 20 ms, release 450 ms) pushes it down a
  further ~8–10 dB and lets it swell back in pauses. Lower to -20 for dense narration; raise to
  -14 for long silent visual beats.

## Effects
- Hard cuts (`"transition": "cut"`) get a soft whoosh (-26 dB) just before the new scene.
  Continuous transitions get nothing: the image doesn't cut, so the sound shouldn't either.
- The intro and outro stings get a quiet pencil scribble as the circle is drawn.
- `sfx: false` turns all of this off. Don't add effects for every animation; a video full of
  pops feels cheap. If one moment deserves a sound (the aha), say so in the plan and add it by
  hand with `music.py sfx` + ffmpeg, but that's rare.

## Loudness
Final mix is normalised with `loudnorm` to -16 LUFS integrated, -1.5 dBTP, LRA 11 (web/podcast
norm). After stitching you can check with
`ffmpeg -i out/video.mp4 -af ebur128=framelog=quiet -f null -` (expect I ≈ -16 LUFS).
