# 2.5D scenes: the explainer-agency look, in Manim

The second waitwhat style. Where the classic style is a 3Blue1Brown blackboard, 2.5D is the look
of a good agency explainer: **flat illustrated vector art** (rounded, friendly shapes, a
pastel-but-punchy palette, gentle gradients), **layered in depth** and seen through a **parallax
camera**, with soft shadows, rim highlights, a recurring character, and **snappy pacing**. It is
pure Manim on the same pipeline: same `ww render`, same narration timing, sound cues, aspects and
lint. Everything here is in the style lib (`from waitwhat_style import *` gives you
`WaitwhatScene25D` and the kit below).

## When it is used

The style is chosen per video by the planner (`plan.style`), never per scene:
- `"classic"`: maths, science, educational and college subjects (the default when unsure).
- `"25d"`: everything else: companies and products, everyday "how/why" questions, history and
  stories, business concepts, how services work.

In a 2.5D video **every scene** is a `WaitwhatScene25D`. No `WaitwhatScene`, no `"dim": "3d"`, no
LaTeX blackboard, no pencil circle: those are the classic style. A formula, if one is truly
needed, goes on a `pill()` or a `big_number()` card.

## The skeleton

```python
from waitwhat_style import *
from shared import MAYA                     # shared.py: MAYA = dict(name="maya") or full Character kwargs

class Problem(WaitwhatScene25D):
    backdrop = "room"                      # outdoors | city | room | office | plain (or plan scenes[].backdrop)
    def construct(self):
        maya = cast(**MAYA, pose="type").stand(x=-1.0)          # feet on GROUND_Y
        table = layer(desk(), "main", depth=1.05)              # a touch nearer: drawn in front of her
        table.move_to([-1.0, GROUND_Y - 0.35, 0], aligned_edge=DOWN)
        self.play(pop_in(maya, table, gap=0.09), run_time=0.6)  # beat 1, at once
        self.wait_until("problem")
        self.play(maya.to_face("worried"), slide_in(*letters, frm=RIGHT, gap=0.1))    # beat 2
        self.wait_until("cost")
        self.push_in(1.25, to=maya, run_time=1.4)              # beat 3: the camera leans in
        self.play(pop_in(pill("6 hours a week", "RED", icon_name="cross")))
        self.finish()
```

Bookmark timing (`until`, `wait_until`, `play_until`, `finish`), `sfx=`, `self.sfx(...)`,
`by_aspect`, `SAFE_*`, `side_by_side`, `place_title` and the layout lint all work as in 2D.

## Visual language

- **Flat fills, no outlines.** Shapes are filled; depth comes from a darker side face
  (`extrude`, `side_shade`), a light rim on the upper-left edge (`rim_light`), and a soft contact
  shadow under everything that stands (`soft_shadow`, a radial gradient). Light comes from the
  upper left, always.
- **Palette**: `plan.theme` in a 2.5D video is one of three palettes: `sunny` (default, warm
  daylight), `fresh` (mint and teal; fintech, health, software), `dusk` (violet evening, lit
  windows; night, calm or dramatic stories). Colour names (`"BLUE"`, `"TEAL"`, `color_of("x")`)
  are fills. `tint(c)`, `shade(c)`, `haze(c)` give the lighter, darker and farther cousins.
- **Text reads on anything**: `pill(text)` (white card, dark ink; or a coloured card with the
  ink picked for contrast), `tag(text, thing)` (a pill with a pointer), `bubble(...)`,
  `thought(...)`, `headline(title, sub)` (top of the frame, fixed), `big_number("2.9%")`. Never
  colour text with a concept colour on the sky. Rounded sans (SF Pro Rounded), bold, 1-5 words.
- **Far is paler**: far layers are hazed toward the horizon colour (the backdrops do it; use
  `haze()` for your own far things).

## Layers and parallax

Every mobject is drawn through its depth layer: `sky` 0 (painted, never moves), `far` 0.2,
`mid` 0.5, `main` 1.0 (characters and props: the default), `near` 1.5 (foreground that sweeps
past), `hud` (fixed on the screen: titles, counters).

```python
layer(mob, "far")                  # tag (returns mob); untagged things are "main"
layer(mob, "main", depth=0.8)      # an explicit depth: a back row, a nearer desk (1.05)
hud(mob)                           # screen-fixed
self.place(mob, layer_name="mid")  # tag and add now; pop_in()/slide_in() add tagged things themselves
```

The camera is `(x, y, zoom)` in the main plane: the point (x, y) is shown at the screen focus
(`SAFE_CENTER`), a layer at depth d scales by `zoom ** d` and follows `d` of every pan. So a pan
moves the far hills a fifth as much as the characters; a push-in grows the foreground faster than
the background; that is the 2.5D feel.

| Call | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `self.pan_to(target, run_time=1.4, zoom=None, added_anims=[...])` | centre a point or mobject; far layers move less |
| `self.push_in(zoom=1.3, to=None, run_time=1.5)` / `self.pull_out(zoom=1.0, to=None)` | move forward / back; zoom 1 to ~1.8 (pull out below 1 only with `backdrop_opts` bleed) |
| `self.dolly(by=RIGHT*3, run_time=2, zoom=None)` | a sideways tracking shot: the parallax shot (pair with `walk()`) |
| `self.drift(RIGHT*0.1, zoom_rate=0.01)` / `self.stop_drift()` | a slow creep through waits; keeps a held frame alive |
| `self.shake(0.08)` | a short impact shake (with a thud) |
| `self.camera_move(to, zoom, by, run_time)` | the move as an animation, to combine in one `self.play` |
| `self.set_camera(to, zoom)` | frame the opening instantly |
| `self.in_view(mob)` | shift a label/bubble so it sits in the safe area from where the camera is now |
| `after(0.7, anim)` | start an animation partway through a play (pop a sign as the pan reveals it) |

Camera moves get a soft swish/whoosh automatically. **Rules**: one move per idea, 1-2 s, eased;
move on the word, then let the narration land; push-ins for "look closer", pans/dollies for "and
then it goes there", a pull-out for "the whole picture". Labels belong to the layer of their
thing (`tag()` does it), so they ride along. Things that interact (a coin travelling to a shop,
a hand holding a phone) must share a depth, or they slide apart during a move.

**Backdrops** (`backdrop = ...` class attribute, or `scenes[].backdrop` in the plan; the scene
builds it in setup): the sky or wall is painted once (free), the scenery comes in layers:

| Backdrop | Layers |
| --- | --- |
| `outdoors` | sky + sun glow; clouds that drift (far); hazed hills (far); hills with trees (mid); curved ground with grass (main); low bushes (near) |
| `city` | sky; skyline silhouettes (far); colourful buildings with lit windows (mid); a wide sidewalk and the road (main); planters (near) |
| `room` | warm wall; window, framed picture, shelf, floor lamp (mid); wooden floor + rug; a plant (near) |
| `office` | cool wall; window with a skyline, pinboard, clock, cabinet (mid); floor; a plant (near) |
| `plain` | soft gradient with drifting colour blooms (far) and floating dots (mid): diagrams, products, comparisons in `panel()` cards |

`self.stage["mid"]`, `["far"]`, `["clouds"]`, `["near"]` are the parts (dim or pop them out).
`backdrop_opts = {"pan": 13}` builds the layers wide enough for a 13-unit camera trip;
`{"foreground": False}`, `{"clouds": 1}`, `{"sun": False}` simplify. `self.set_backdrop("city")`
switches place mid-scene (prefer a cut and a new scene).

Ground and size: `GROUND_Y` is where feet stand, `HORIZON_Y` the horizon, `CHAR_H` a standing
person (3.3 in 16:9, 5.3 in 9:16). Props stand with `.move_to([x, GROUND_Y, 0], aligned_edge=DOWN)`;
things nearer the viewer stand a little lower (GROUND_Y - 0.3) and a little larger.

## Characters

`cast(name)` gives a ready person; the same name gives the same person in every scene. Put the
protagonist in `shared.py` once (`HERO = "maya"`, or full `Character(...)` kwargs) and use it
everywhere: **one character stays visually consistent across the video**.

| Cast | Look |
| --- | --- |
| `maya` | tan skin, black bun, teal tee |
| `leo` | light skin, brown short hair, orange hoodie |
| `sam` | brown skin, black curls, purple shirt |
| `ana` | fair skin, long auburn hair, pink tee |
| `kai` | deep skin, black buzz cut, gold hoodie |
| `zoe` | light skin, blonde bob, blue jacket |
| `ravi` | tan skin, side-parted black hair, glasses, green shirt |
| `nora` | brown skin, long dark hair, red jacket |

`Character(skin=light|fair|tan|brown|deep, hair=short|side|bun|long|bob|curly|buzz,
hair_color=black|dark|brown|auburn|blonde|grey|pink|blue, top="TEAL", style=tee|hoodie|shirt|jacket,
bottom=hex, shoes=hex, glasses=False, height=CHAR_H, pose=..., face=...)` for anyone else.

- Place: `.stand(x)` (feet on the ground line), `.head_point()`, `.hand_point("R")`.
- Poses (`.to_pose(name, run_time=0.45, hold=prop)`): `stand`, `point`, `point_left`, `think`,
  `type` (at a laptop: stand them behind a `desk()` with `laptop(back=True)`), `phone`,
  `celebrate`, `wave`, `shrug`, `hold`, `present`. `hold=` puts a prop in the hand (sized to it);
  `.hold(prop)` does it at once.
- Faces (`.to_face(name)`): `neutral`, `happy`, `joy`, `worried`, `sad`, `surprised`,
  `thinking`, `determined`. Glances: `.look((1, 0))`.
- They blink and breathe by themselves. Motion: `walk(char, to_x)`, `hop(char)`, `nudge(char)`.
- Don't run a pose change and a `pop_in`/`travel` on the same character in one play (both drive
  its points); pose and face changes together are fine.

## Props and icons

All flat, shadowed, rim-lit, standing on their base (`aligned_edge=DOWN`):
`phone(screen=app|pay|chat|check|chart|map|blank)`, `laptop(screen=..., back=False)`, `desk()`,
`sofa()`, `house()`, `shop(sign="CAFE")`, `bank(sign="BANK")`, `building(w, h)`, `skyline()`,
`coin()`, `card()`, `cash()`, `chart(values)` (`.bars`), `envelope()`, `cloud()`, `tree()`,
`pine()`, `bush()`, `hill()`, `server()` (LEDs flicker), `gear()` (turns), `bulb()` (glows),
`clock(spin=True)`, `plant()`, `lamp()`, `window()`, `route(a, b)` (a dashed path),
`panel(w, h, kind)` (a card holding a little world: comparisons, insets).
Icons: `icon(name, size, color)` and `badge(name, color)` with `check cross plus heart star lock
pin bell chat user bolt cart search arrow dollar wifi shield home globe`.
Shape helpers for your own props: `rrect`, `capsule`, `blob`, `extrude`, `side_shade`,
`rim_light`, `soft_shadow`, `glow`, `merge` (many same-coloured shapes as one: cheaper), `tint`,
`shade`, `haze`, `on_color`. Phone/laptop screens: `.ui` (`.ui.button` on `pay`).

Idle life (drawn by the camera, free of conflicts): `idle(mob, "bob"|"float"|"drift"|"sway"|
"breathe"|"blink"|"pulse"|"flicker"|"spin")`, `still(mob)`. Clouds drift, trees sway, screens
pulse; add one to the thing the viewer should keep looking at.

## Motion and pacing

**A new visual beat every 2-3 seconds.** A beat is something appearing, changing or moving on a
word: a pop-in, a pose or face change, a camera move, a coin flying, a number swapping. A frame
should never sit still for more than ~3 s; idle life covers the gaps, it does not replace beats.

| Preset | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `pop_in(*mobs, run_time=0.5, anchor="bottom"\|"center", gap=0.07)` | the default entrance: from small with a ~6 % back-out overshoot, opacity leads |
| `pop_out(*mobs)` | exits, quicker than entrances (0.28 s) |
| `slide_in(*mobs, frm=LEFT, distance=1.2)` / `slide_out(...)` | arrivals from a side on the glide curve (letters, cards, a character walking in) |
| `stagger(*anims, gap=0.08)` | a burst of things in quick order |
| `nudge(mob)` | the stressed word's beat: a swell and settle |
| `hop(mob)` | a jump with squash and stretch (joy, landing) |
| `travel(mob, to, arc=0.8, spin=0)` | something going somewhere: money, a message, a parcel |
| `walk(char, to_x)` | walking with a step bounce |
| `swap(old, new)` | replace in place |
| `burst(at)` | confetti (success; once or twice a video) |
| `ping(mob)` | the 2.5D "look here": soft rings |

Curves: entrances `glide` (easeOutQuint) and `back_out`, camera `ease_io`, exits `leave_ease`.
Typical run_times: pops 0.4-0.6 s, slides 0.5 s, camera 1-2 s, poses 0.45 s. Prefer several short
plays on bookmarks over one long one.

Sound: pops and slides get soft pops and swishes automatically, camera moves a swish or a whoosh.
Add meaning with `sfx=`: `coin` (money), `register`/`payment` (a payment goes through), `click`
(a tap), `notify`/`message`, `approve`/`stamp`, `wrong`, `chime` (the aha, once), `paper`.

## 9:16 framing

The lib adapts GROUND_Y, CHAR_H (5.3: people are bigger), the sun and the cloud spots. Then:
- Two characters side by side at most (x ≈ ±1.6); a third goes in the back row or a later beat.
- Rows in depth instead of rows across: a back row at depth 0.8 on `GROUND_Y + 0.5`, a front row on
  the ground; back-row names above, front-row names below (`tag(..., DOWN)`).
- Flows run sideways with the camera (a dolly between stops 7-8 units apart) or stack in
  `panel()` cards (`side_by_side` stacks them).
- Titles at the top on the sky (`headline`), nothing to read in the bottom 15 % (the ground and
  the near layer live there). After a push-in, `self.in_view(label)` before showing a label.

## Performance budget

Measured on this machine (Cairo, 1080p30, one scene at a time):

| Scene | Render | Video | Shapes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| classic `comparison.py` (for reference) | 2.9 s | 8.7 s | - |
| `story_problem.py` 16:9 / 9:16 | 4.9 / 5.2 s | 19 s | ~190 |
| `city_overview.py` 16:9 / 9:16 | 6.0 / 5.6 s | 16 s | ~210 |
| `service_flow.py` 16:9 / 9:16 (two long dollies, wide city) | 7.4 / 6.9 s | 17 s | ~320 |
| `before_after.py` 16:9 / 9:16 | 8.2 / 8.5 s | 15 s | ~260 |

The cost is shapes on screen × seconds redrawn (~15 µs per shape per frame). Idle life and camera
moves redraw every frame, so a 2.5D scene always costs a little more than a still 2D one. Rules:
- **≤ 450 shapes on screen** (`SHAPE_BUDGET`); a backdrop is 60-150, a character ~45, a prop 3-15.
- **Merge repeats**: windows, dashes, dots, tufts: `merge(...)` (or a `Union(...)` of them).
- **Big radial glows are the priciest fills**: at most a few per scene (the sun, a bulb, a halo).
- `backdrop_opts={"pan": N}` only as wide as the camera really travels (wider = more buildings).
- A scene should render in 4-10 s; the lint reports "render budget" past ~14 s
  (`logs/<id>.layout.json` has `perf`: `shapes_max`, `redraw_s`, `est_render_s`).

## Pitfalls

- **Mixing depths**: a coin in `main` travelling to a shop at depth 0.8 drifts off it during a
  camera move. Keep interacting things at one depth.
- **z-order** follows the layer (far < mid < main < near < hud), then insertion order. Things
  that must be in front of a character at the same depth (a desk) get depth 1.05.
- `pop_in` adds the mobject; `self.add` is for things already there at the start.
- `self.finish(fade=True)` (before a cut) pops out the characters, props and labels and keeps the
  backdrop; never `FadeOut(*self.mobjects)` yourself (the camera rig is one of them).
- Labels after a camera move: compute positions in world units; `self.in_view()` fixes the rest.
  The lint flags labels half cut by the frame edge; a label fully out of view is fine.
- Don't `Write`/`Create` text in 2.5D (that is the pencil look): pop it in on a pill.
- `pull_out` below zoom 1 shows past the backdrop's edge unless `backdrop_opts` has a big enough
  `pan` (each unit of zoom-out needs ~2 units of pan reach).
- A scene id equal to a Python module (`code`, `random`, `types`) breaks the import: `card-flow`, not `card`.
